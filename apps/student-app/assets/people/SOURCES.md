# Demo comment portraits

Photographic placeholders for fictional prototype accounts; these are not
verified photos of the named students. Images are bundled for offline display.
Each student keeps the same assigned portrait across questions and replies.

Source: Material UI's public avatar examples, used with the repository's MIT
license (preserved in LICENSE-MUI.txt).

- student-02.jpg: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mui/material-ui/master/docs/public/static/images/avatar/4.jpg
- student-03.jpg: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mui/material-ui/master/docs/public/static/images/avatar/2.jpg
- student-04.jpg: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mui/material-ui/master/docs/public/static/images/avatar/5.jpg

Downloaded unchanged; the existing Avatar component supplies its usual circular
crop at the existing size. No live third-party photo requests are made by the app.
